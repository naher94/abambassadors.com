  In hospitality real estate, value is rarely obvious at first glance. In this episode of Hospitality Talks, Jimmy Merkel, CEO and co-founder of Rockbridge, shares how a lifelong curiosity about investing grew into a disciplined approach to building durable value across hotels and hospitality assets. From his early lessons in value investing to the mentors who shaped his leadership philosophy, Jimmy reflects on the experiences that helped define Rockbridge’s long-term perspective. He also discusses how the firm evaluates market cycles, repositions complex assets, adapts to changing consumer preferences, and builds trust with lenders, investors, and teams. Beyond the numbers, Jimmy offers a thoughtful look at purpose-driven leadership, community impact, and what it takes to lead with integrity in a constantly evolving industry.
