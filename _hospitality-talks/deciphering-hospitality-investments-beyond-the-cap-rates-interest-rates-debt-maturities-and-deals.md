description: |
  Summary:
  Dan Peek, President, Americas, JLL Hotels & Hospitality, discusses how higher interest rates, debt maturities, and shifting capital flows are reshaping hospitality M&A and deal-making. He explains why institutional capital remains selective, how foreign investors are approaching U.S. hotel markets, and why cash flow continues to drive investment decisions. The conversation also explores urban market recovery, value-add opportunities, the future of economy hotels, and the importance of sophisticated capital strategies in a complex market. Dan closes by reflecting on his own career path and emphasizing hospitality as a resilient, long-term career platform for emerging leaders.
  
  Key Takeaways:
  • The current M&A environment is evolving with new interest rates.
  • Institutional capital is underallocated to real estate, particularly hospitality.
  • Foreign capital is diversifying, with a focus on specific markets.
  • Cash flow remains a critical factor in investment decisions.
  • Urban markets are recovering, with Chicago leading the way.
  • Valuations are becoming more favorable for transactions.
  • Shared experiences are becoming more valuable in the hospitality sector.
  • The transaction market is heating up, indicating a positive trend.  The traditional hotel market is recovering, but challenges remain for mid-tier franchises.
  • The cost to replace hotel assets is historically high, reinforcing the value of existing properties.
  • Luxury hotel assets command premium cap rates due to their unique positioning.
  • Private equity remains a significant player in the hospitality investment space, though they are cautious.
  • The economy hotel model is facing challenges but remains a vital part of the market.
  • Investors must navigate complex capital structures and market dynamics to succeed.
  • Labor supply and costs are critical issues affecting the hospitality industry today.
  • Hospitality offers diverse career opportunities, challenging the perception that it is a temporary job. 
  • Perseverance and resilience are key traits for success in hospitality.
