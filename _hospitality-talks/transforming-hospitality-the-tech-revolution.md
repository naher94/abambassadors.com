  Summary
  In this conversation, Afsheen Afshar, Founder and Managing Partner of Pilot Wave Holdings, describes his journey from biomedical engineering and neuroscience into AI and private equity. He explains why empathy, measurable ROI, and user experience are essential to successful technology rollouts, and how data can improve business decisions while enabling greater personalization in hospitality. Afsheen and Abid also examine cultural inertia, the disconnect between leadership and technology, the need for technologists to understand business priorities, and the ethical implications of collecting and using consumer data. They conclude by considering how to create a more consumer-centered data economy, evaluate technology against business outcomes, and identify practical opportunities for innovation in hospitality.
  
  Takeaways
  • Empathy is a key differentiator in technology rollouts.
  • Quantifying ROI is essential for successful technology investments.
  • Consumer experience can suffer due to poorly executed technology.
  • User experience improvements lead to better technology adoption.
  • Data is a critical asset for businesses, often overlooked.
  • Understanding workflows is crucial for leveraging data effectively.
  • Technology should serve business goals, not the other way around.
  • Hyper-personalization remains a challenge in the hospitality sector.
  • Organizations must prioritize user experience to improve technology adoption, while recognizing that cultural inertia affects every industry.
  • Leadership often lacks a deep understanding of technology.
  • Technologists must learn to communicate in business terms.
  • Transparency with consumers about data usage is crucial.
  • Consumers are willing to trade privacy for value.
  • The data economy needs to be inverted for consumer benefit.
  • Evaluating technology should focus on ROI, not just implementation.
  • There are significant opportunities for improvement in hospitality.
  • AI trends may be overhyped without measurable benefits.
