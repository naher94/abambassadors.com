  Summary
  
  
  In this engaging conversation, Abid Butt and Barbara Witoff, Partner at JOI-Design, delve into the profound impact of design in the hospitality industry. They discuss how design goes beyond aesthetics to shape guest emotions and behaviors, creating memorable experiences that drive loyalty and brand distinction. Barbara reflects on her path into hospitality design and explains how thoughtful spaces shape guests’ emotions and first impressions. She explores the roles of storytelling, authenticity, and local character, as well as the challenge of balancing these elements with brand standards. The discussion also examines changing design trends, demographic influences, evolving public spaces, sustainability, and the use of local materials. Barbara closes by considering the commercial realities of design, including budget constraints and sharing lessons from her work across land-based hospitality and cruise ship interiors.
