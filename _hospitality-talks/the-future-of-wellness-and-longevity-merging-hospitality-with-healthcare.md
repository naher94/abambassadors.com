  Summary
  In this conversation, Ingo Schweder, Founder and CEO, GOCO Hospitality and Managing Director Horwath HTL Health & Wellness, reflects on the personal health crisis that moved him from traditional hospitality into wellness and shaped his approach to GOCO Hospitality. He discusses the evolution of the wellness industry, the growing convergence of hospitality and healthcare, and the importance of personalized, culturally relevant wellness experiences. The conversation also explores shifting consumer psychology, generational differences in technology use, gender dynamics in wellness, the rise of community-driven experiences, and the need for a more balanced future that integrates scientific innovation with natural practices.
  
  Takeaways
  • Ingo's personal battle with cancer shifted his perspective on health.
  • The wellness industry is rapidly evolving, focusing on longevity.
  • Experiential travel is becoming a key aspect of wellness.
  • Not every resort needs extensive wellness facilities.
  • Cultural relevance is crucial in wellness offerings.
  • Personalized wellness is essential for effective health management.
  • Consumer demand is driving scientific advancements in wellness.
  • Younger generations are more aware of their health needs.
  • Data overload can lead to stress and burnout.
  • A democratic approach to wellness is necessary for all.  
  • Pharmaceutical companies contribute to rising healthcare costs.
  • Healthy eating habits are crucial for wellness.
  • Education is key to understanding wellness needs.
  • Loneliness is a growing concern across all generations.
  • Festivalization of wellness promotes social interaction.
  • Wellness communities foster engagement and connection.
